### Added
- `herdr-mods/ovis-assistant`, a herdr-app mod with the Assistant as Code mode shows it: its chat, and
  beside it Environment, Context, Changes, Sessions (a click opens that session's chat), Background
  tasks and Restart Assistant. `@` tags a session above the box, and Voice opens the Assistant's voice
  in the browser. It finds the Assistant on the Ovis's PCs by itself.
- A link with `?assistant=voice` opens the Assistant's pane in voice.
