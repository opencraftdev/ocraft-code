### Added
- A PC's bridge can run on herdr-core, the engine of herdr's desktop app, with
  `OVIS_ENGINE=herdr-core` (`OVIS_HC_BIN` names `hc` when it is not on the PATH). Its spaces, tabs
  and splits show as workspaces, tabs and panes, with live agent status, attached terminals and
  typing. Changing the layout from the browser comes later; herdr 0.9 stays the default.
