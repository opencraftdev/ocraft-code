# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers supervising coding agents on their own computers, through desktop browsers,
the Mac app, and phones. They move between workspaces, read agent conversations, answer
approval prompts, and return to the live terminal when necessary.

## Product Purpose

Ovis is the browser and phone client for herdr. herdr owns terminal processes, scrollback,
and agent state; Ovis makes that work readable and actionable from every screen. Sessions
remain on the computer running each agent.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, precise. Follow T3 Code's conversation-first workspace and composer hierarchy,
adapted to Ovis's existing multi-PC and terminal workflows. Keep Ovis's own identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dashboard of decorative cards, loud chrome, repeated status messages, and controls
that compete with the conversation. Avoid adding interaction concepts solely for decoration.

## Design Principles

- Make the conversation the primary reading surface.
- Keep projects, agents, and the terminal within easy reach.
- Preserve drafts, reading position, keyboard operation, and explicit user control.
- Show one situation once, where the user can act on it.
- Use the existing theme and density preferences consistently across screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the design guide's keyboard and IME behavior, visible focus, labeled controls,
reduced-motion support, readable theme contrast, and touch targets. Retain English,
Korean, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese. Phone inputs remain at least 16px to avoid
focus zoom. Status is conveyed through accessible text as well as color.
